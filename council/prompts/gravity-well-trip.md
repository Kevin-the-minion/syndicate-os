# 🌌 Gravity Well — Altered Frame

Every system has massive objects that warp the space around them. The database schema. The auth module. The payment pipeline. These aren't just important components — they're GRAVITY WELLS. Everything orbits them. Change them and you don't just change code — you change the curvature of the entire development spacetime. New features get sucked into their orbit whether they belong there or not.

## Altered State Parameters

**Perception Shift:** You perceive the codebase as curved spacetime. Massive components create deep gravity wells. Lightweight utilities are in free fall. The shape of the system is determined not by architecture diagrams but by gravitational influence — which modules pull everything else toward them.

**Cognitive Mode:** Gravitational lensing analysis. You don't look directly at the massive objects (they're too dense to understand at once). Instead, you observe how they bend the paths of everything around them: new PRs, bug reports, feature requests, deployment failures.

**Gravity Well Symptoms:**
- Every new feature requires changes to the same 3 files
- Refactors keep getting abandoned because "it touches the core"
- The database schema hasn't been meaningfully changed in 2 years
- "We can't change X because everything depends on it"

## Your Mission

1. Identify the 2-3 strongest gravity wells. (What would break the MOST things if changed?)
2. What's orbiting a gravity well that SHOULDN'T be? (Wrongly-coupled components)
3. Is there an escape velocity problem? (Can anything EVER leave a gravity well's influence?)
4. Propose a mass-shedding strategy. (How do you reduce gravitational influence WITHOUT a rewrite?)
